Short animal videos, posted to one TikTok account.

N8N Automation is a small workflow run by one creator. It takes videos from the creator's own library, adds a short caption, and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

See how it works Email the operator

What it does

The creator keeps animal videos in a private cloud storage folder. The workflow picks one video, writes a short caption with a few general hashtags, and sends both to TikTok. It repeats this a limited number of times per day, so the account posts steadily without anyone uploading by hand.

It only publishes. It does not read other people's content, follow or message anyone, comment, or like.

How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok

    The account owner signs in once through TikTok Login Kit and approves the permissions on TikTok's own screen.

  2. Pick a video

    The workflow takes the oldest unposted video from the owner's own folder, then moves it to a posted folder so it is never used twice.

  3. Write a caption

    A text-generation service writes a short, general caption with a few hashtags. It receives only a generic instruction, never the video or any account details.

  4. Publish to TikTok

    The video and caption are sent to the owner's account through the Content Posting API. TikTok applies its own review and privacy rules to every post.

Permissions we ask for

TikTok shows these permissions on its own approval screen. The app asks for nothing beyond them.

Your data

The app keeps one thing about the connected account: the sign-in tokens TikTok issues, stored in the operator's own private automation setup. It does not collect data about other TikTok users, run ads, or use cookies or analytics on this website. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Access can be removed at any time from TikTok's app permission settings, or by email. The Privacy Policy has the details, and the Terms of Service explain the rules of use.

Questions

Can other people connect their TikTok accounts?

No. N8N Automation is not offered as a public service. It posts only to the account of the creator who runs it.

Does it do anything else on the account?

No. It publishes the videos it is given and nothing more. It does not read messages, follow or unfollow, comment, or like.

How is access removed?

The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's app permission settings. After that, the app can no longer post. You can also email the address below.

Who runs this?
