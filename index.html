N8N Automation

Automated publishing of short animal videos to a creator's own TikTok account.

What it does

N8N Automation is a workflow tool run by an individual creator. It takes short, pre-selected pet and animal videos from the creator's own library, adds a short caption with hashtags, and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in once with TikTok Login Kit and approves access.
  2. A video from the owner's own library is selected and a short caption is prepared.
  3. The video is published to the owner's TikTok account through the Content Posting API.

Your data

The app only keeps the access token needed to post to the account that approved it. It does not collect information about other TikTok users and does not sell or share data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
